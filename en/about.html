What is GearDrop?

GearDrop at geardrop.100app.dev is an independent outdoor deal discovery and price-tracking service for public listings across retailers and markets.

Content reviewed: 2026-09-08
Short answer

GearDrop does not sell products and is not an official website of Arc'teryx, Burton, Patagonia, or any listed retailer. Orders, payments, shipping, returns, and final availability remain with the retailer.

What GearDrop provides

  • A searchable catalog of public Arc'teryx, Burton, and Patagonia listings.
  • Retailer source, market, currency, original URL, and observation time where available.
  • An iPhone app for saved products, price history, and price-drop alerts.

What GearDrop is not

  • It is not a retailer, rental marketplace, giveaway, or map game.
  • Listing a brand or retailer does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or authorization.
  • A recorded price or stock signal is not a guarantee of checkout availability.
