Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

GearDrop helps shoppers discover outdoor gear markdowns, compare recent price history, save products, and create price alerts. This policy covers the GearDrop iOS app and the website at geardrop.100app.dev.

Information we store

  • Saved products, saved-time price snapshots, item and model alert settings, and notification preference are stored locally on your device.
  • If you separately choose a one-time email alert for an individual item, GearDrop stores the email address you enter, target price, product SKU, current price, region, product URL, image URL, and an unsubscribe token. Editing or deleting an on-device alert does not delete this email subscription; use its emailed unsubscribe link.
  • When you contact support, GearDrop stores your email address, subject, message, language, request status, and timestamps so the request can be answered and managed.
  • Apple processes Pro purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, device and operating-system information, App Store receipt and purchase history to validate transactions, restore purchases, and provide Pro entitlement status.
  • GearDrop does not ask for account passwords, payment card numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or health data.

Information we read

The app reads public product and price-history data from the GearDrop catalog. Product records can include merchant product names, prices, regions, product URLs, and images. The app does not read other users' price-alert subscriptions.

Notifications and purchases

Local notifications deliver on-device item and model target-price alerts and can also be used for alert testing. Remote push notifications are not enabled. Optional item email alerts are separate and do not require iOS notification permission. Pro purchases are processed by Apple through in-app purchase and validated by RevenueCat.

Tracking and sharing

GearDrop uses short-lived pseudonymous request fingerprints to rate-limit public forms and prevent abuse. GearDrop does not implement third-party advertising tracking. RevenueCat processes purchase information for app functionality and subscription analytics; it is not used for advertising tracking. If you tap a Buy link, the product page opens in your browser and the merchant may process that visit under its own privacy policy.

Retention and control

Device-local watchlist data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. RevenueCat retains purchase and entitlement records under its service policies; App Store purchases remain associated with your Apple ID. Email alert records, including delivered one-time alerts, remain stored until deleted through their emailed unsubscribe link or removed during service maintenance. Support requests remain until they are resolved and removed under service-maintenance procedures. Abuse-prevention fingerprints are automatically pruned by the service.

Contact

For app help, privacy questions, or data requests, use the GearDrop support form.
